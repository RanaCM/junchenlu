I study how machines can speak with the timing, rhythm and feeling of a human voice. My doctoral work formulated *Automatic Voice Over*, a multimodal task in which video guides text-to-speech, moving from matching lip motion to matching facial expression. Lately I have been carrying that work out of the lab, building real-time voice systems that ship to real users.
